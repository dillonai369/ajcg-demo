1185 Surrey Dr, Glen Ellyn, IL · 6-Unit Multifamily

Property Overview

AJ Commercial Group is pleased to present 1185 Surrey Drive, a fully updated six-unit multifamily property located in the highly desirable suburb of Glen Ellyn, Illinois. This stabilized asset offers a strong unit mix, significant recent capital improvements, and rare off-street parking — positioning it as a compelling turnkey investment in one of DuPage County's most sought-after rental markets.
