2091 & 2121 Best Place

2091 & 2121 Best Pl, Aurora, IL · 16-Unit Multifamily Portfolio

Property Overview

AJ Commercial Group is proud to announce the sale of 2091 & 2121 Best Place, a 16-unit multifamily portfolio in Aurora, Illinois. This off-market opportunity featured two adjacent all-electric 8-unit buildings with stable tenancy, operational efficiency, and multiple paths for income growth.

Located in one of the fastest-growing corridors of the western suburbs, the asset offered both scale and upside in a supply-constrained rental market.

Property Highlights

  • ✓Two side-by-side 8-unit buildings
  • ✓Full basements with on-site laundry
  • ✓Dedicated carports at 2121 Best Pl
  • ✓Below-market rents in several units
  • ✓Recent mechanical upgrades including newer water heaters
  • ✓Separately metered electric; landlord covers water, trash, common areas
  • ✓Well-located near schools, major employers, and retail corridors in Aurora
