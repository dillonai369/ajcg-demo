About Joey

Joey is the Co-Founder of AJ Commercial Group and an active multifamily investor with extensive experience in the Chicago multifamily market. A former collegiate golfer, Joey developed the discipline, focus, and competitive mindset that now define his approach to brokerage and investing. Known for his expertise in underwriting, deal analysis, and market knowledge, he helps clients understand both the current value and long-term potential of their assets.

Before co-founding AJ Commercial Group, Joey was part of one of the highest-producing multifamily brokerage teams in Chicago, where he played a key role in analyzing transactions and contributing to significant sales volume across the market. His analytical approach and deep understanding of multifamily assets have made him a trusted advisor to property owners and investors throughout Chicagoland.

About Anthony

Anthony Munoz is the Co-Founder of AJ Commercial Group and is known for his strong sales ability, relationship-building skills, and relentless approach to multifamily brokerage. Before launching the firm, Anthony was a top-producing broker at his previous brokerage, helping drive significant multifamily sales volume across Chicagoland.

Prior to real estate, he built a strong foundation in sales and client relations through his experience in medical device sales with Stryker. Anthony was also a collegiate wrestling All-American, a background that helped shape his competitive mindset, discipline, and work ethic. His ability to connect with owners, negotiate effectively, and consistently create opportunities has helped AJ Commercial Group rapidly grow into a respected name in Chicagoland multifamily real estate.
